About CodeTribe

Technology is better when we build it together.

A practical, people-first tech conference for curious minds in Kimberley and beyond.

Why we gather

Make room for better questions.

CodeTribe Tech Conference brings developers, designers, founders, students and technology enthusiasts into the same room for two focused days of learning and connection.

We believe the best ideas grow through honest conversations, hands-on practice and a community willing to share what it knows. Every session is designed to leave you with something useful to take back to your team, your studies or your next big experiment.
